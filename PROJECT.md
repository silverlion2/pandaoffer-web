# PandaOffer

PandaOffer is a Next.js product for international students and professional groups exploring China through university matching, application guidance, study tours, and practical student tools.

The core matching journey is resilient by design: valid profiles receive either AI-assisted results or a transparently labeled shortlist derived from the repository's curated university dataset. External AI and Supabase enrich the experience but are not allowed to become a hard availability gate for the basic matching result.

Current product decisions live in `docs/product-spec.md`, system boundaries in `docs/architecture.md`, and priorities in `docs/roadmap.md`.
